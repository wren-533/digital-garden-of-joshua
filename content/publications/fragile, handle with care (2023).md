(Self-) published as I was wrapping up my senior year of high school, "fragile, handle with care" is an anthology of poetry penned by myself and my friends. Chock full of teenage angst and the turmoil of self-discovery, my writing in "fragile, handle with care" is a far cry from what it is today. While I much prefer my poetic voice nowadays, I look back fondly on this book—for what it lacks in nuance or subversion, I take pride in the fact that I put something of my own out into the world, simply for the reason that I wanted to. 

If you dare to read, you can pick up a copy on [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/fragile-handle-care-anthology-poetry/dp/B0C527FWCX/ref=sr_1_6?crid=2ZHWKZY3XHYX9&keywords=fragile,+handle+with+care+book&qid=1684541754&sprefix=fragile,+handle+with+care+book,aps,141&sr=8-6) (or if you know me personally, just ask for one—I have plenty in backstock!). Check out the blurb below:
> *“fragile, handle with care” is about the things left unspoken. (Unreliably) narrated by five teenagers coming of age in an age of pandemics, protests, and polarization, this anthology walks the precarious tightrope of fine detail vs. big picture, self vs. society, subconscious vs. reality.*
> 
> *Through a hodgepodge constellation of seven chapters linked by little more than crossed fingers, “fragile, handle with care” unapologetically broaches questions surrounding family and culture, self-actualization and change, love and sexuality, grief, and the obscure interior of the mind. Offering a collection of hypotheses, half-truths, and “shots in the dark”, “fragile, handle with care” intends to be a self-aware mirror for the bittersweet reader: an acknowledgement that it could all shatter to pieces—and that that’s okay.*
